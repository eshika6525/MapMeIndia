# 🌏 MapMeIndia

**MapMeIndia** is a fun, interactive geography quiz game that challenges players to guess famous Indian locations using Google Street View. Think of it as a desi take on GeoGuessr — perfect for anyone who loves exploring places and testing their memory of Indian landmarks.

---

## ✨ What It Does

- 🗺️ Shows real Google Street View scenes from iconic places in India.
- 🔤 Lets players guess the location by typing its name.
- ⏳ Includes a countdown timer (20 seconds) to add thrill.
- 💯 Tracks your score — 10 correct answers to win, 3 wrong to lose.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Hit “Start Game” — you’ll be dropped somewhere in India.
2. Look around in Street View and try to recognize the place.
3. Type your guess and hit **Submit** before the time runs out!
4. Earn points for right answers. Three wrong guesses? Game over!

---
